- **No monthly plans.** The offer works on 1 year and 3 year hosting, and 1 year and 2 year VPS. Monthly billing is not covered.
- **New purchases only.** Promotional pricing is for new purchases and does not extend to upgrades on existing plans.
- **Free domain terms.** The free domain covers the first year, and standard rates apply from year two. If you cancel within 30 days and received a free domain, its standard price is deducted from your refund. You keep the domain.
- **Refund windows.** Shared and WordPress plans have a 30 day money back guarantee on first purchases, and VPS plans have 7 days. Renewals and monthly plans are not covered. Read the [refund policy](https://www.youstable.com/refund-policy).
- **Plan limits.** DaStart covers one website. Shared plans follow the [Fair Usage Policy](https://www.youstable.com/fair-usage-policy), so a very busy site may need a VPS.
